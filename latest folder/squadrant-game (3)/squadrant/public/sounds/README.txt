SQUADRANT — Audio Asset Folder
================================

Place your MP3 files here to enable music:

  menu.mp3        — Plays on Main Menu (looping background music)
  game.mp3        — Plays during gameplay (battle music, auto-switches)

Place your MP4 here for LAN matchmaking loading screen:

  matchmaking.mp4 — Fullscreen background video during room searching

NOTES:
- Without these files, procedural Web Audio (synthesized) plays instead
- Files must be royalty-free or original compositions
- Recommended: 128-192kbps MP3, stereo
- Music fades in/out automatically (no abrupt cuts)
- Volume controlled separately from SFX in Settings
